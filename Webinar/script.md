## Section 3 — The Three Secrets (3:00–9:00, ~2 min each)

Each Secret follows Brunson's pattern: **break a false belief → install a new belief → mini proof.**

### Secret #1 — Vehicle belief: *"It's not about MORE training. It's about TRACKED training."*
- **Old belief (the lie):** "If my athlete just trains more / goes to more showcases, they'll get noticed."
- **Why it's broken:** showcases are one weekend, no follow-through, no data trail. More reps with no record = invisible reps.
- **New belief:** the training your athlete already does, *captured*, becomes a recruiting asset that compounds.
- **Mini-proof:** show the RFX session-capture flow on screen (30 seconds of footage).

### Secret #2 — Internal belief: *"You don't need to be a 'recruiting expert' parent or DOC."*
- **Old belief:** "I don't know which coaches to email, what video to send, what stats matter — so I just hope our club handles it."
- **Why it's broken:** most clubs aren't built to handle individual recruiting at scale. Most parents don't have the playbook.
- **New belief:** RFX builds the recruiting profile *for* you — the athlete profile, share link, and outreach scaffolding are already there.
- **Mini-proof:** show the auto-generated athlete profile + share-with-coach flow.

### Secret #3 — External belief: *"College coaches aren't ignoring you — they can't find you."*
- **Old belief:** "If my athlete is good enough, the right coach will find them."
- **Why it's broken:** college coaches are drowning. They don't have time to scout — they need athletes delivered to them in a format they can evaluate in 90 seconds.
- **New belief:** RFX puts your athlete in front of coaches in exactly that format. **TODO:** confirm the exact RFX coach-facing surface name (Coach View / Recruiting Feed / etc.).
- **Mini-proof:** screen of the coach-facing view.

## Production notes
- Record once, evergreen forever — avoid dated references ("this fall," "2025 season," etc.).
